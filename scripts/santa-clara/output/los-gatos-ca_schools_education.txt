**Schools and Education in Los Gatos**

Los Gatos, California is home to a diverse educational landscape that supports families with a broad range of needs and aspirations. The local school districts, which serve the community, offer various programs and resources aimed at fostering student achievement through public, charter, and private educational options. Families can access information regarding enrollment processes and attendance boundaries directly from district resources to ensure they have the most accurate and current details.

For residents interested in higher education, Los Gatos is conveniently located near several colleges and universities that provide opportunities for both traditional and continuing education. Additionally, public libraries and community centers in the area often offer enriching after-school activities that can complement a child’s learning outside the classroom.

• Verify school district boundaries and enrollment eligibility on official district websites.  
• Contact local schools to inquire about curriculum offerings and extracurricular opportunities.  
• Explore private and charter school options in the area, as they may have different admission processes.
